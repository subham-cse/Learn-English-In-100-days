# Learn English in 100 Days

Welcome to **"Learn English in 100 Days"**, a comprehensive, structured English learning program engineered to take learners from **beginner to advanced professional fluency** in a systematic, step-by-step manner.

Every single day includes:
- **`notes.md`** – An exhaustive micro-lesson featuring learning objectives, in-depth theoretical breakdowns, vocabulary/grammar rules, realistic conversational dialogues, exercises, complete self-check answer keys, and practical daily tips.
- **`quiz.html`** – A self-contained, responsive, modern interactive quiz testing that day's concepts with live timer, progress tracking, instant answer validation, and detailed pedagogical explanations.
- **`mega_quiz_phaseX.html`** – At the conclusion of each 20-day phase (Day 20, Day 40, Day 60, Day 80, and Day 100), an extensive **30-Question Milestone Mega Quiz** with an interactive 30-button question palette, countdown timer, instant grading, and pedagogical answer reviews.

---

## 🎯 100-Day Program Goals & Milestone Quizzes

- 🧱 **Phase 1: Days 01–20 (Basics & Vocabulary):**
  - Master core parts of speech, sentence word order, regular/irregular verb conjugations, descriptive language, quantifiers, spatial prepositions, and everyday survival expressions.
  - 🏆 **Milestone Capstone:** [Phase 1 Mega Quiz (30 Questions)](Day20/mega_quiz_phase1.html)

- ⚙️ **Phase 2: Days 21–40 (Grammar & Sentence Structure):**
  - Gain deep command over the English tense system (aspectual contrasts, perfect tenses), articles, partitives, modals of deduction, conditionality (Zero through Mixed), passive voice, and reported speech.
  - 🏆 **Milestone Capstone:** [Phase 2 Mega Quiz (30 Questions)](Day40/mega_quiz_phase2.html)

- 🗣️ **Phase 3: Days 41–60 (Idioms, Phrasal Verbs & Expressions):**
  - Master separable/inseparable and three-word phrasal verbs, high-yield idioms of effort, business, time and emotion, natural collocations, easily confused pairs, and diplomatic conversational discourse markers.
  - 🏆 **Milestone Capstone:** [Phase 3 Mega Quiz (30 Questions)](Day60/mega_quiz_phase3.html)

- 📖 **Phase 4: Days 61–80 (Reading & Comprehension):**
  - Develop advanced reading velocity and critical analysis through micro-stories, technical documentation, editorials, scientific abstracts, financial statements, legal agreements, literature, and speed reading strategies.
  - 🏆 **Milestone Capstone:** [Phase 4 Mega Quiz (30 Questions)](Day80/mega_quiz_phase4.html)

- ✍️ **Phase 5: Days 81–100 (Speaking & Writing Practice + Capstone):**
  - Perfect executive business communication, escalation emails, structured argumentative essays (PEEL framework), resume tailoring, STAR interview techniques, boardroom presentations, debate frameworks, connected speech phonetics, and lifelong immersion practices.
  - 🏆 **Graduation Capstone:** [Phase 5 & Final Capstone Mega Quiz (30 Questions)](Day100/mega_quiz_phase5.html)

## 📌 Daily Study Workflow

1. Open the folder for your current day (e.g., `Day06`, `Day24`, `Day89`).
2. Read the structured lesson in `notes.md` (study theoretical tables, examples, and dialogues).
3. Complete the practice drills and check your understanding with the self-check key.
4. Launch `quiz.html` in any web browser for a timed self-assessment with instant feedback.
5. At every 20-day milestone (Days 20, 40, 60, 80, 100), launch `mega_quiz_phaseX.html` for a 30-question diagnostic exam.
